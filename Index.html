About InAmigos Foundation

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh and working across India to create lasting social impact. the NGO operates nationwide to support underprivileged communities through welfare, education, environmental, and empowerment projects.Key InitiativesProject Seva: Provides meals, clothing, and essential items to vulnerable and needy people. Project Bachpanshala: Supports children with school education, basic digital literacy, and life skills.Project Jeev: Focuses on animal welfare, including daily feeding and stray animal rescue.Project Udaan: Promotes women's empowerment via skill training, self-help groups, and financial independence.Project Prakriti: Drives environmental conservation through tree plantation drives and eco-friendly practices.Project Vikas: Offers training and internship opportunities to youth to build work employability.Credentials & RecognitionLegal Status: Registered as a Section 8 company under the Indian Companies Act.Certifications: Holds 80G and 12A tax exemption approvals, CSR-1 registration for corporate partnerships, and ISO 9001:2015 certification.Government Portal: Registered with NITI Aayog's NGO Darpan.Inamigos FoundationInamigos Foundation: HomeOur Key Initiatives 1.
